About Fit With Friend

We believe understanding your body shouldn't feel clinical or intimidating. Fit With Friend turns basic health numbers into something clear, friendly, and genuinely useful.

Fit With Friend started as a simple student project: a BMI and calorie calculator that didn't feel like a spreadsheet. We wanted something that respected the person using it — clear numbers, honest explanations, and no medical jargon.

Today it's still small and still improving, built around one idea — health information should be easy to understand and easy to act on, whether you're just curious about your BMI or planning your daily calorie intake.
